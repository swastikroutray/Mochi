# Mochi interactions

- Hover over Mochi: petting/purring animation + floating heart.
- Click: short playful hop and reaction message.
- Drag: Mochi is picked up after a 6 px movement threshold and follows the cursor.
- Double click: special “zoomies” reaction.
- Move the cursor over the pet: eyes/head look left, right, or up depending on cursor position.

All of these are implemented in `script.js` and `style.css`.
